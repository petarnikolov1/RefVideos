# RefVideos — BG Referee Watch

A news site for refereeing in Bulgarian football — efbet Liga (1st League), 2nd League, 3rd League and the Bulgarian Cup — with match videos.

## Features

- **Stories, not duplicates.** Articles about the same incident (for example a red card in Левски – Дунав) are grouped into one story. Headlines are compared word by word, with rare words (names, towns, sums) counting most; two headlines need at least two words in common, and articles about different clubs, rounds, divisions or competitions are kept apart, so unrelated stories are not mixed. A story shows one headline article, from the best-known outlet that covered it (Sportal, Gong, Blitz, Topsport, bTV, Nova, BNT, 24 Chasa, Dnevnik first), with up to three other articles listed under it and the rest one click away. Exact re-posts are marked **Duplicate**.
- **Compact filters.** The news opens with one row: search, **Filters** and ↻ Refresh. **Filters** opens the division / competition and topic chips, the order and the Only new / Only hot / Hide read options (it shows how many are on, and stays open or closed on that device). Whatever is switched on shows as a pill under the search row; a tap on a pill removes it, **Clear all** removes everything. The World sections stay visible as one bar. The header is a single slim row (on a phone the tabs scroll sideways), so the stories start near the top of the screen.
- **Bulgaria / World split.** Foreign football never appears in the Bulgaria feed. The **World** tab has three sections: **National teams** (World Cup, Euro, Nations League, qualifiers, Copa América, AFCON, Bulgaria's national team), **European cups** (Champions League, Europa League, Conference League) and **Top 5 leagues** (England, Spain, Italy, Germany, France), each with its competitions as filters. Stories about Bulgarian clubs in the European cups (Левски, ЦСКА, Лудогорец…) and Bulgarian referees abroad (Гидженов in the Europa League or the Nations League…) show in both tabs: in Bulgaria under **Europe & abroad**. Other foreign leagues are left out, including Russian football: "ЦСКА" next to Краснодар, Зенит or Москва is CSKA Moscow, not the Bulgarian club, so those stories are dropped even when written in Bulgarian. Articles in Russian, Ukrainian, Serbian or Macedonian are left out too, recognised by letters Bulgarian doesn't have or by Russian-only words ("которая", "матч", "судья"…).
- **Videos from articles.** The site reads each story's articles and embeds the videos it finds: YouTube (including lazy players), Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Vimeo, Dailymotion, Vbox7, Streamable, a news site's own player, and plain video files. It also lists the match minutes the articles mention, tagged (penalty, red card, offside, VAR, goal…), on the card and in the story.
  - Google News links are resolved to the original article. If Google refuses, the same headline is found through Bing. Sites that block servers are read through public relays.
  - When an article can't be opened, the story says why, and **↻ Look for videos again** retries.
- **ebfu.net.** Every Bulgarian story shows the match, date, division and the situations (minutes, or the type of incident) with a button to the ebfu.net video archive. Each member uses their own ebfu.net login. Jumping straight to the match and minute will be added once the archive's links are known.
- **Read marks.** Stories you've opened show **✓ READ**, and you can hide them. They're stored in the browser and, when logged in, saved to your account.
- **Accounts.** Anyone can read the news and the results. The members' videos and their comments are for registered members approved by the admin (the admin approves members on the **Admin** page).
- **Members' videos.** On the **Videos** page, or straight from a story, approved members add clips:
  - **A video file.** A screen recording of about 5 minutes is fine. The free database plan allows 50 MB per file, so anything bigger (or in another format) is compressed in the member's browser first: H.264 MP4 up to 720p at 30 fps, with the bitrate worked out from its length, with a progress bar. This needs a recent Chrome, Edge or Safari.
  - **Links, one per line.** YouTube, Facebook, Vimeo, Vbox7, Instagram, TikTok, and **Google Drive**. A Drive file plays on the page. A whole **Drive folder** (shared as "Anyone with the link") lists every video in it, subfolders included; the member unticks any they don't want and adds the rest in one go, each titled by its file name. Drive has no size limit, so it is also the way for full-quality or long videos.
  - Videos are sorted into **Bulgarian videos / World videos**, then **Approved by the Referee Committee / Not checked yet**, then the **Laws of the Game** categories: Offside, Handball, Serious foul play & violent conduct, SPA & DOGSO, Penalty-area incidents, challenges, Holding & pulling, Simulation, Advantage, Cautions & send-offs, VAR interventions, Game management, Laws of the Game application, Disputes & mass confrontations, Positioning, Teamwork, Other. A video can be in several categories.
  - **The admin** adds, renames, reorders and deletes categories (**⚙ Manage categories**); approves a video for the Referee Committee with the Committee's comment, moves it between Bulgarian and World and ticks its categories (**⚙ Sort this video**); and ticks several videos to **copy** them to a category, **move** them (out of the category being viewed, or out of all their categories) or change their section or status at once.
  - Uploaded files are stored privately and played through short-lived signed links, so only members can watch them.
- **Comments.** Approved members can read and write comments on the members' videos. The author or the admin can delete a comment.
- **Results and table.** Sections **България**, **Топ 5 лиги**, **Евротурнири** and **Национални отбори**. Every round of the 2026/27 season of efbet Лига, Mr Bit Втора лига and Трета лига Югозапад. For the first two: both team logos, the score and half-time, and for each played match Gong.bg's short report (**▶ Репортаж**) and extended report (**▶ Разширен**), played right in the match card, plus reports from other channels under **▶ Други**. Many 2nd-league matches have no Gong report, only a club's clip or nothing at all. The page opens on the latest round; ‹ › and the round picker move between rounds.
  - **News for every match.** Under each match card a thin coloured strip says how many articles were written about that match (**📰 5 статии · 2 теми**, with the main headline); a tap opens them right there, grouped into stories exactly as in the news feed (headline article, the other articles under it, **Details & video**, **Mark read**). A match with nothing written shows a faint dashed line instead. This works for the Bulgarian leagues and every world competition. In the news feed, each story shows the match it is about (**⚽ Левски София – ЦСКА София · 13 кръг**); a tap opens that round on Results with the match's news open.
    - How a story finds its match: each headline is read on its own. A headline naming both teams goes to their match (a week before for appointments, up to nine days after for the aftermath). A headline naming one team goes to that team's last match for decisions, cards, complaints and fines (3 days, 5 for Committee and disciplinary rulings), to its next match for referee appointments (up to 5 days ahead), otherwise to the nearer of the two. All competitions count together, so "ЦСКА" two days after a Conference League away game goes to that game, not to the league match before it. A headline naming a fixture that isn't on the Results page (a cup tie, a 1st-League v 2nd-League match) or a foreign club ("Панатинайкос – ЦСКА") is only matched accordingly, and "between two of Levski's rivals" doesn't count as Levski.
  - **Обзор на кръга.** At the top of every played round: the round's review show (Diema's "Обзор на кръга", uploaded by Gong.bg to YouTube), for both leagues. It plays on the page, or opens on YouTube when the owner allows only that.
  - **Referees on every match.** Each match card of efbet Лига, Mr Bit Втора лига and the Third League shows its referee (**Съдия**) and, in the first two leagues, the VAR; **Бригада** opens the whole crew (assistants, fourth official, VAR and AVAR, the referees' observer, the delegate) with a link to the committee's post. Names are shown as first and last name (the committee writes first, father's and last name: "Никола Антонов Попов" → "Никола Попов"). The committee writes its posts in two layouts — a table with one cell per role and name, or paragraphs with several roles to a line ("ГС: … АС1: … АС2: …") — and both are split into one official per role. The appointments are read from the Referee Committee's posts (bfunion.bg for the 1st and 2nd League, bfu-tournaments.com for the Third League), which usually come out 1–2 days before the round; until then a match in the next four days says **Съдиите за мача още не са обявени**. If the committee changes an appointment, the card follows.
  - **Трета лига Югозапад.** The Third League South-West has its own section next to the two professional leagues: every round's results (with half-time, the venue, postponed matches and awarded results), the fixtures, the table with the last five results, and the referees. No videos are searched for it.
    - **Punishments.** After each round the Disciplinary Committee's decisions: the suspended players (name, team, 5th yellow card or red card with the article, how many matches) and, folded away, the fines and bans for clubs and staff, with a link to the PDF. **Всички наказани играчи през сезона** lists the whole season. On the match cards, **Наказани** names the players who sit that match out (a ban of N matches is served in the team's next N matches).
  - **Table.** Under the round's matches, the current table of the same league: games, wins, draws, losses, goals, goal difference, points and the last five results.
    - **What each place leads to.** The place number is coloured and a bar marks the row on the left, with a legend under the table: European places (Champions League blue, Europa League orange, Conference League green, lighter for the qualifying rounds), promotion green, play-offs, and relegation red. The Bulgarian leagues follow the 2026/27 rules: in **efbet Лига** 1–4 go to the championship group, 5–8 to the Conference League group and 9–14 to the relegation group after the 26th round (at the end: 1st → Champions League qualifiers, 2nd–3rd → Conference League qualifiers, 12th–13th → play-off against the 2nd League's 2nd–3rd, 14th relegated); in **Втора лига** 1st promoted, 2nd–3rd promotion play-off, 13th relegation play-off, 14th–18th relegated, with the reserve teams (II) skipped for promotion; in the **Third League South-West** the leader (not a reserve team) is promoted, 12th–16th may go down depending on how many clubs drop from the 2nd League, 17th–18th relegated. The world competitions use ESPN's notes for each place (European places and relegation in the big leagues, round of 16 / play-offs / out in the UEFA league phase, promotion and relegation in the Nations League).
  - **World competitions.** The Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga and Ligue 1; the Champions League, Europa League and Conference League; and the national-team competitions — World Cup, Euro, Copa América, Africa Cup of Nations, Nations League and their qualifiers. National-team competitions are listed only while they (or their qualifiers) are being played: during a World Cup or AFCON its groups and knockouts show, otherwise the qualifiers or the Nations League. Results and tables come from ESPN; rounds are worked out from the fixture list (a postponed match stays in its round), group stages show one table per group, and a knockout stage (play-off, 1/8-финал…) is one page with both legs.
- **International media for the World sections, in English.** Besides the Bulgarian outlets, the World stories are searched on Google News (UK and US English editions) in the big English-language media (BBC, Sky Sports, ESPN, The Guardian, The Athletic, The Telegraph, Reuters…) and in the official English editions of the big foreign outlets and league sites (AS English, MARCA in English, SPORT English, Mundo Deportivo, Football Italia, ANSA English, DW, France 24, RFI, beIN SPORTS, LaLiga, Lega Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1). Only those outlets are kept, and only headlines written in English (`looksEnglish` in `intl.js`: a site that also writes in Spanish, Italian, German or French needs an English word in the headline). The refereeing rules are the same as before (a referee, VAR, a penalty or red card decision, a disallowed goal, the referees' bodies — PGMOL, CTA, AIA, the DFB's "Kölner Keller", the DTNA — bans and fines). Second tiers, women's football, other sports, betting tips, live blogs and penalties that were only taken or saved are left out. Each such article shows **EN** next to the outlet, and its headline is matched to its match on Results too ("Arsenal v Liverpool", "Levski Sofia" in the Europa League).
- **Searching (on the server, kept).** The news is searched on Supabase, not in your browser, and every story found is saved. Every 30 minutes the last few days are searched again (about 80 Google News and Bing News searches, a third of them for the World sections: each big league and its main clubs, Premier League referees, the Negreira case, the three European cups and the national teams). In the background the whole season is gone through once a day, 2 weeks at a time, so stories published or indexed late are added too. Nothing found is searched again from scratch. The page opens instantly from the saved stories; **Refresh** reloads them.
- **NEW.** Stories found since your last visit are marked **NEW**, including older-dated stories that were only found now. Stories are sorted by date, newest first (or by importance).
- **Refereeing only.** A story is kept only when its headline is about refereeing: referees and their appointments, decisions (penalties, red and yellow cards, offsides, disallowed goals, VAR, fouls and handballs), the Referee Committee, disciplinary sanctions and fines, attacks on referees, fights and incidents, match fixing, and complaints about refereeing. Transfers, contracts, injuries, coaching changes, interviews, live match blogs and penalties that were only taken or saved are left out unless the headline itself is about a refereeing decision.

## Results and Table — how they stay current

The Edge Function **football-sync** runs on Supabase on a schedule. The site only reads the saved data, so the pages open instantly.

- **Results, fixtures, tables:** at :02 and :32 every hour, from efbetleague.com (1st league) and mr-bit.bg (2nd league).
- **Videos:** every 5 minutes, up to 10 played matches are searched on YouTube. A match still missing a Gong report is retried after 3 hours, 12 hours, a day, then every 3 days for its first 10 days; an older match is searched once. Searches use the short club names Gong uses in its titles (Левски, Славия, Локомотив София…), and a Gong title with a mistyped score is still accepted when its round is right. A video is only accepted when its title names both teams with the home team first, any score, round or season in it agrees with the match, and it isn't older than the match. Analysis shows and interviews are skipped.
- **Обзор на кръга:** with the results job (every 30 minutes) up to 3 YouTube searches look for the review of rounds that have been mostly played and don't have one yet: right away, then after 3, 6, 12 and 24 hours, then daily, up to 12 searches; a round more than two weeks old is searched twice (Gong posts the show a day or two after the round, later around international breaks). Titles like "Обзор на кръга в efbet Лига (14 кръг, 3.11.2026)" or "Обзор на кръга в Mr Bit Втора лига (14 кръг, 04.11.2026)" are matched by league and round; a title with only a date is matched to the round that ended just before it. One search usually finds several rounds at once. "Героите на кръга", "Преди кръга" and last season's shows are skipped.
- **Gong first, others separately.** **▶ Репортаж** and **▶ Разширен** are always Gong.bg's reports, in both leagues, whenever Gong has them. Every other video of the match (the league's channel, clubs, media and other uploads whose title names both teams with the right score or round) is listed under a third button, **▶ Други**.
- **Playable here or only on YouTube.** Some owners (Gong.bg among them) block their videos from playing on other sites. Every video is checked with YouTube, and a blocked one opens on YouTube when its button is clicked. Under every video that plays on the page there is a **Гледай в YouTube** button.
- If a result is corrected, its videos are cleared and found again.

- **World competitions:** at :12 and :42 every hour **world-sync** reads last, this and next month of every competition from ESPN, and the tables of the ones being played (with what each place leads to); once a day (04:20 UTC) it reads their whole season.

- **Referees and the Third League:** at :25 and :55 every hour **refs-sync** reads the Referee Committee's newest posts (bfunion.bg for efbet Лига and Втора лига, bfu-tournaments.com for the Third League) and again every post of the last 10 days, in case an appointment was changed; each appointment is matched to its match by both clubs' names (written in many ways: "ПФК Славия ||", "ФК Пирин Разлог 1941", "ПРОФЕСИОНАЛЕН ФУТБОЛЕН КЛУБ ЦСКА ЕАД"), the round and the kick-off. The same job reads the Third League's results and table from bulgarian-football.com and the punishments PDF of each new "Наказания" post.

To redeploy the functions: `supabase functions deploy football-sync --no-verify-jwt` (and the same for `world-sync`, `news-sync`, `refs-sync`).

## Cloudflare Pages

1. **Workers & Pages** → **Create application** → **Pages** → **Connect to Git** → GitHub → **RefVideos**.
2. Production branch `main`, Framework preset **None**, Build command empty, Build output directory **`public`**.
3. **Save and Deploy**. Every push to `main` redeploys automatically.

## Supabase (accounts and video uploads)

The site uses the **RefVideos** Supabase project (Frankfurt). The database, security rules and the `videos` storage bucket are already set up from `supabase-setup.sql`. The project URL and publishable key are at the top of the script in `public/index.html`; the key is meant to be public, and the data is protected by the security rules.

Still to do in the Supabase dashboard:

1. **Authentication → URL Configuration:** set **Site URL** to the site's address (for example `https://refvideos.pages.dev`) and add the same address under **Redirect URLs**, so confirmation and password-reset emails link back to the site.
2. Register on the site, then make that account the admin. Run `update public.profiles set role = 'admin', approved = true where email = '…';` in the SQL Editor.

New members start as **waiting for approval**. Approve them on the site's **Admin** page.

To use a different Supabase project, run `supabase-setup.sql` there and change the two values in `index.html`. Alternatively, clear them and set `SUPABASE_URL` / `SUPABASE_ANON_KEY` as Cloudflare Pages variables.

## Running it locally

Open `public/index.html` in a browser. News, results and accounts come from Supabase; video finding needs the Cloudflare worker.
